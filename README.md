Offline-first calisthenics coach. Real-time on-device pose tracking draws a skeleton over your body while you train, counts reps, calls out form faults, and replays your set with plain-language feedback after you stop. Minimalist true-black design. Everything runs on-device — no account, no network required.

### what it does

- Live skeleton overlay during your set (squat, push-up, plank, lunge, glute bridge)
- Automatic rep counting and real-time form correction
- Post-set replay with a plain-language form report
- Full workout history, stored locally
- Zero accounts, zero tracking, zero network calls

Shipped to TestFlight. Actively in development.
